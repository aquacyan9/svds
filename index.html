Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to some of the common questions we receive.

What documents are required for enrollment?

Generally, you will need to provide a copy of your Aadhar card or other valid address proof, age proof (like your birth certificate or SSC certificate), and passport-sized photographs. We will provide a detailed list upon inquiry.

Do you offer weekend classes?

Yes, we understand the busy schedules of many individuals and offer weekend classes to accommodate your needs. Please inquire about the availability of weekend slots when you contact us.

What types of vehicles do you use for training?

We have a well-maintained fleet of modern and learner-friendly vehicles, including both manual and automatic transmission cars, as well as various two-wheeler models to suit different learning preferences.

How do I book a driving course?

You can book a driving course by contacting us via phone or email. You can also visit our office during working hours to discuss your requirements and enroll in a suitable batch.

What is the duration of each driving session?

Typically, each practical driving session lasts for about 45-60 minutes. The duration may vary slightly depending on the course and the instructor's assessment of your progress.

Do you provide pick-up and drop-off services for training?

Currently, we do not offer dedicated pick-up and drop-off services. Training sessions usually commence and conclude at designated meeting points near our office. Our team will provide you with these details upon enrollment.
